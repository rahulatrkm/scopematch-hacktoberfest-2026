ScopeMatch / Open weights. Private briefs.

A small tool for the business of freelancing

A better first quote.
Not another cloud inbox.

Turn a short client brief into a service shortlist and a reviewable price range. Open-weight AI finds the match. Your own rates do the math.

01 / Understand the request

What does your client need?

20-800 characters, in English. Use a focused summary, not personal data or credentials.

Your brief stays in this tab.First use downloads the public AI library and model from jsDelivr and Hugging Face (tens of MB). Inference is local. No account, API key, analytics, or brief storage.

Ready when you are. The model loads only when you analyze a brief.

A human stays in charge

Intake, without the guesswork.

  1. Match the intent. MiniLM compares the brief with six transparent service descriptions.
  2. Keep pricing explainable. Starter hours x your rate, plus your chosen buffer. AI never invents a price.
  3. Review before sharing. Choose a scope, ask the follow-up questions, and export a plain-text draft.

Built for freelancers and small studios that sell repeatable services. The catalog is illustrative, not a promise about how long your client's work will take.

02 / Review the shortlist

The closest service matches

Similarity is a cosine score, not a probability or a guarantee. Check each scope before choosing. Price ranges exclude taxes, subscriptions, and third-party costs.

These are low-overlap matches (top cosine score below 0.30). This brief may be outside the catalog. Clarify the work before choosing any scope.
